# Server Rack

Model: https://grab3d.com/models/electronics/server-rack/
Original full-quality GLB: https://grab3d.com/dl/server-rack/server-rack.glb
License: CC0-1.0, as stated by Grab3D on the model page.

The original download is unchanged. Placement, uniform scaling, day/night PBR lighting and real-time self-shadowing are implemented in src/server-rack-replacement.js. The cabinet front faces the room (+Z), clear of the window ledge, the desk and Kirby. Only authored piano vertices are excluded, using piano-vertices.json, generated by tools/export_piano_removal_mask.py; floor geometry is never selected by a bounding box.

The parquet is rebaked in Blender with the piano hidden and the rack in its actual position (tools/bake_rack_floor.py), then denoised with Blender's compositor (tools/denoise_rack_floor.py). tools/package_mint_materials.cjs replaces only that parquet UV island, removing the original piano's stale shadows and matching the approved original wood tone on well-lit pixels. No large shadow-overlay plane is used. The original room asset remains recoverable in Room_Portfolio.glb.

Layout adjustment: the rack center is (1.65, 0.527, -2.94), height 3.45 scene units. Kirby advances by +0.90 on exported Z (Blender Y -0.90), with its geometry, materials and hover behavior unchanged. Both surface bakes include these final positions. The rack's rear is at Blender Y 3.8595; the window ledge starts at Y 3.961, leaving about 0.10 scene units of clearance. The new footprint also clears the chair and the floor lamp.

Walls: run both bake and denoise scripts with RACK_SURFACE=wall to rebake only Cube's Room-material UVs without the old piano. Wood trim and the Outlet material are excluded from the replacement mask; the approved mint wall tone is matched from the existing material-edited atlas.

Procedural cracks: rebuild_mint_materials.py disconnects the Room shader's normal link from the Wall cracks frame, retaining the color/plaster detail. The walls are then rebaked, not covered with an overlay.

The microphone formerly resting on the piano is removed from the live scene before hover/raycast registration. Both surface-bake modes hide the source Microphone object as well, so its baked shadows are removed. Original source geometry remains recoverable.

The user-identified sustain pedal with its tall disconnected cord (source object Plane.067, merged into Third in the web asset) is included in the authored-vertex removal mask and hidden in both surface bakes. Wire_Two, the wall-outlet cable removed through an earlier misunderstanding, is restored. The wall outlet and all other cables are retained.
